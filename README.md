# No.36 - FAIL: Self-Orthogonal Cellular Automata

Ryutaro Yonezu / Yonezu Research Lab / archived exploration / 24 October 2026.

**STATUS: FAIL / candidate rejected. GitHub archive only. No Zenodo registration. No novelty or impossibility theorem is claimed. All rights reserved.**

This records the author's decision to stop a diameter-seven binary bipermutive cellular-automaton candidate and retain the failed exploration publicly. The historical reason given was prior overlap. The strongest cited overlap claim could not be source-verified when preparing this archive and is explicitly qualified in PRIOR_ART.md. FAIL means the candidate was not advanced as a new research result; it is not a proof that nonlinear examples never exist.

The considered local form was f(x1,...,x7)=x1 XOR x7 XOR g(x2,...,x6). The target property is that the Latin square produced by the associated no-boundary CA be orthogonal to its transpose. The established theory and linear characterization belong to Mariot and Mazzone, cited below. The paired no-boundary maps may be viewed through a periodic global map; this wording does not redefine the original no-boundary model.
